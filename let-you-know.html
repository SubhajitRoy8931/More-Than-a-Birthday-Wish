THE COMPLETE ORIGINAL POEM

Let You Know

Do you know I am afraid of you?

I am afraid to talk to you

I am afraid when you come to me

I am afraid to lose you

I am afraid to ask you Would you love me?

But today I have made my mindset

To let you know everything I feel for you

You asked me once what your dreams are

I answered something that’s just a few

My dreams will only fulfil

When I will make you mine.

The day I see you first

Was the day I fall in love with you

The charm that you have

Have made me deaf, dumb and blind

From the dusk to dawn, from day to night

You are always in my heart as well as my mind.

The way you smile, the way you blink your eyes

The perfect way is to martyr me in love

Whenever you talk to me

My heart starts beating faster,

Whenever I see you in front of my eyes

My heart automatically flies.

Love is not something to see

It’s mean everything to me,

Love is not something to hear

It always wanting to have you near,

Love is not something to taste

It’s the journey from miss to Mrs.

This was never what I intended

I just wanted to be away of this

But when I fall for you

I forgot all the commitments that I made

And started loving you from the bottom of my heart

Now I have made you my desire, my love, my life.

I know I am not perfect for you and never will be,

But I promise you one thing

No matter where life will take us

I will never leave or give up on you

I will always stay by your side and

Assure you everything will be okay.

No words can say no action can express

The way I feel about you

There’s nothing I could ever say and nothing I could do

To let you know just how much I love you

A love so precious, a love so true,

A love that comes from me to you.

You may think I love you

But actually, I love my heart that is with you.

SUBHAJIT ROY
