Aakhri Phool

Tere liye jo aag jalayi thi, aaj usi ki raakh se apna anjaam likh raha hoon,

Jis mohabbat ko umr bhar jeena tha, aaj usi ka aakhri paigaam likh raha hoon.

Ek tuition ki mulaqat se baaton ka silsila chal pada,

Gyarah ko tere janamdin par pehla paigaam bheja, dil ko raasta mil gaya.

Chaubees ko dil ka haal kaha, pachchees ko tera iqraar mila,

Ek anjaan se chehre mein phir jeene ka ek khwaab mila.

Shehron ke faasle the, mulaqatein kam, par intezaar beshumaar raha,

Kabhi chand message mein din guzra, kabhi khamosh video call mein izhaar raha.

Paanch saal jis mulaqat ko khwaabon mein sanjoya tha,

23 January ko Tufanganj mein woh pehla khwaab haqeeqat hua.

Phir Holi ke rangon mein ek ankahee shikayat ghul gayi,

Meri ek bhool tere dil mein khamosh si doori ban gayi.

Main maafi ki dehleez par umeed liye khada reh gaya,

Nau March ko paanch saal ka saath alag raaston pe chal diya.

Main judaai ko hi kahaani ka aakhri panna samajhta raha,

Apne hisse ka maatam khamoshi mein odhta raha.

Phir pehli June ko jaana, pandrah din baad tera naya rishta ban gaya,

Jis pal ko main anjaam samjha, tere liye woh naya aaghaz ban gaya.

Main abhi alvida ke har harf ka matlab samajh raha tha,

Udhar tere kal ka pehla panna kisi aur ke naam khul raha tha.

Calendar ne pandrah din gine, maine ek umr ka janaaza dekha,

Tere faisle pe haq na tha, par maine khudko bikharna toh dekha.

Paanch saal jis Tufanganj ki shaam ko khwaab bana kar rakha,

Tune wohi shaam kisi aur ke saath ek hafte mein jee li.

Jis awaaz ke liye main lamhon ki mohlat maangta raha,

Woh ab kisi aur ke roz mein shaamil huwa.

Jo mere saath mushkil tha, tere aaj mein aasaan ho gaya,

Main teri tabdeeli se nahi, apne intezaar se hairaan ho gaya.

Maine jise umr bhar ki mohabbat samjha, tune use kashish kaha,

Ek hi kahaani thi, maine umr likhi, tune guzra hua waqt kaha.

Socha tha tere saath chehron ke parde uthaunga,

Kya khabar thi tere aaine mein apna bharam paaunga.

Duniya se pehle meri nazron ka aitbaar toot gaya,

Jis aks ko sach maana tha, usi mein mera khwaab chhoot gaya.

Har behas ke baad khamoshi ko sulah samajhta raha,

Teri baat sambhaalne ko apni awaaz dabaata raha.

Tere ishare the, ya meri aankhon pe yaqeen ka parda tha,

Main ghar bachaata raha, deewaron mein daraar badhti rahi.

Tu kehti thi meri umeedein tere kandhon pe bojh ban gayi,

Meri kuch khwahishein tere liye zanjeer ban gayi.

Main chahta tha tu kaamyaab ho, meri koi shart nahi thi,

Teri manzil par meri mohabbat ka koi qarz nahi tha.

Haan, jism ki khwahish thi, isme kya gunaah kamaya?

Par teri 'na' ke aage maine kab apna haq jataya?

Saala, jism hi manzil hota toh paanch saal wafadaar kyun rehta?

Phir ek khwahish ke naam pe mera wafaa ko kyun bhulaaya?

Jis saanp ne dasa, main usi se ilaaz poochta raha,

Zehar andar utarta raha, main usi se sabab poochta raha.

Marham mere hi paas tha, main nishaanon ka hisaab karta raha,

Khud ko bachana tha, main tujhse insaaf maangta raha.

Ab har sawaal ka jawaab tere dar se nahi maangunga,

Har raat apne zakhmon ko tere naam se nahi jagaaunga.

Main tujhe maaf karta hoon, magar dard ko jhooth nahi kahunga,

Jo sach mein toota tha, use salamat dikhane ki kasam nahi khaunga.

Ab meri taraf se tere dar pe koi dastak nahi hogi,

Teri yaad toh aayegi, magar tujhe pukaar nahi hogi.

Tujhe bhoolne ka jhootha daawa bhi nahi karunga,

Par tere intezaar mein ab ek aur umr nahi guzarunga.

Kabhi dhalti hui roshni ghar lautne ka ehsaas thi,

Aur ab wohi roshni raat ke aane ka dard hai.

Kuch shaamein umr bhar yaadon mein roshan reh jaati hain,

Unhe yaad rakhna khoobsurat to hota hai par unmein theharna saza ban jaata hai.

Bees se jo khwaab dekhe the, chhabbees mein unhe dafan kar raha hoon,

Jis kal mein tera naam likha tha, us kal ko aaj azaad kar raha hoon.

Aaj aakhri phool laaya hoon, har pankhudi mein ek dard liye,

Tere liye nahi, humari qabr ke liye, humare beete kal ke liye.

Jin khwaabon ko tune aankhon mein chupke se paala hai,

Dua hai har ek khwaab ko apna aasmaan mile.

Teri mehnat ka har qatra ek din rang le aaye,

Tu jis manzil ko chhoo le, woh tere naam ho jaaye.

Tere ghar ki dehleez pe sukoon yun thahar jaaye,

Jaise thaki hui shaam ko aakhir apna ghar mil jaaye.

Teri aankhon ki roshni pe koi mausam bhaari na ho,

Teri hansi ko khilne ke liye koi wajah zaroori na ho.

Main apni gardish tere aasmaan se hata raha hoon,

Na paigaam ban ke lautunga, na kisi mod pe milunga.

Tu bekhauf apna kal chun, mere lautne ka darr na ho,

Meri umeedon se teri rihaai hai, ab tujh par mera koi qarz baaqi na ho.

Jis gyarah ko tere janamdin pe pehla paigaam bheja tha,

Chhe saal baad usi gyarah ko apni taraf se aakhri salaam bhej raha hoon.

Tu meri yaadon mein rahegi, magar mere intezaar mein nahi,

Tera janamdin har saal aaye, mera naam uske saath aaye ye zaroori toh nahi.
